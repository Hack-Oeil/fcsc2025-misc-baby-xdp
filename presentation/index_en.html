Challenge #546

Baby XDP

Where did my network packets go?

In this challenge, we invite you to play with XDP.

An attacker has set up a covert communication channel between an infected machine and the network. Can you retrieve the content of the packets received on the network interface? tcpdump does not seem to work…

Notes:

- In order to have a working Ctrl-c, you can use stty -cooked -echo on your terminal after backgrounding your challenge connection made with nc. - To reproduce the challenge locally: Extract the archive with tar xf babyxdp.tar.xz. Start the Docker container with docker compose up --build. Connect to the local challenge with nc localhost 4000. - To log in to the machine, use the password "user"
